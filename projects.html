ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

BuildWiseAI

AI-Powered Construction Assistant

An AI-powered assistant designed to help users understand construction-related information.

BuildWiseAI helps users analyze building documents, inspect construction images, estimate preliminary construction costs, and search for construction market information.

It uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to answer questions from provided building documents. OCR helps extract text from scanned documents, while LangSmith supports tracing and monitoring the AI workflow.

Technologies: Python, FastAPI, Streamlit, LangChain, ChromaDB, Groq

Features: Document Analysis, Visual Inspection, Cost Estimation, Construction Market Search, RAG, OCR, and LangSmith Tracing.
